# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and welcoming for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and collaborative.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Welcome constructive feedback and different viewpoints.
- Protect the privacy and dignity of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or insults.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that prevents productive collaboration.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable conduct through a private GitHub message to the repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially where possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions, or temporarily or permanently block participants whose behavior is harmful or inconsistent with this code.
